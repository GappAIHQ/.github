# GappAI

**Industrial Physical AI for real-world work.**

GappAI transforms workers’ operational expertise into deployable Physical AI — across humanoid platforms.

## Capture → Skills → Workforce

- **Capture:** Transform expert human workflows and real-world operations into structured training data.
- **Skills:** Develop reusable, measurable and platform-independent robot skills.
- **Workforce:** Deploy and improve Physical AI across humanoid platforms in real operational environments.

## Industrial focus

- Manufacturing & Assembly
- Industrial Operations
- Logistics & Infrastructure

## What we are building

GappAI is building the infrastructure required to capture operational expertise, develop reusable robot skills, evaluate performance and deploy Physical AI safely in the real world.

We work with industrial companies, robotics manufacturers and research institutions to move humanoid robotics from demonstration to productive operations.
